# Privacy policy

FTC Ultimate Inventory is made by HuskerTheCat for FTC robotics teams. This page says what the app shares with its developer and what it doesn't.

## What the developer receives

- **When a new team is set up:** the team name, program (FTC, FRC, FLL or Other) and team number, the team's Firebase project ID, a random key that only lets the team's owner take the team off the list, and the date. The app looks up the project ID by program and team number so teammates can sign in on a new device, so anyone with the app can find a team's name and project ID from its number.
- **When a team owner deletes the team in the app:** the program, team number and the date.
- **When someone sends a bug report:** the report text, the team name, the app version and the date. Please don't put personal info in a report.

That's all. The developer never receives names, emails, passwords, parts lists or anything else about a person or a team's inventory.

## Your team's data

Accounts, parts, projects and the change log are stored in your team's own Firebase project, which your team admin set up and controls. The developer can't see it. Firebase is run by Google under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Removal and questions

To have your team's name and number removed, or to ask anything about this policy, open an issue at [github.com/HuskerTheCat/FTC_Ultimate_Inventory/issues](https://github.com/HuskerTheCat/FTC_Ultimate_Inventory/issues) or send a bug report from the app.

The app uses no cookies, ads or tracking scripts.
