## What gets logged to the central server

- **When a new team is set up:** the team name, program, team number, Firebase project ID, a security key, and the date. Anyone with the app can look up a team's name and Firebase project ID by its program and team number, which is how teammates sign in on a new device.
- **When a team owner deletes the team:** the program, team number, the date, and the owner's reason if they write one.
- **When someone sends a bug report or feature request:** the text, team name, app version and date.

Please don't put personal info in reasons, reports or requests.

That's all. Names, emails, passwords, accounts, parts lists, projects and anything else about a person or a team's inventory are never sent to the developer's server. They stay in your team's own Firebase project, which your team admin controls. The developer can't see them.

**Firebase is run by Google under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).**

## Supplier photos

Catalog part photos load directly from the supplier's website (goBILDA, REV, AndyMark, ServoCity, TETRIX, Studica, SWYFT, Axon, Limelight or The Thrifty Bot), so the supplier can see your device's IP address, like any website. The app sends them nothing else.

## Removal and questions

To have your team's name and number removed, or to ask about this policy, [open an issue](https://github.com/HuskerTheCat/FTC_Ultimate_Inventory/issues) or send a bug report from the app.

The app uses no cookies, ads or tracking scripts.
