# What's new

The app's ☰ → What's new shows every `## ` section here, newest first. A blue dot shows on ☰ until a user opens What's new after the top section changes.

Before running **Build apps**, add the version's section at the top, e.g. `## v0.6.4 · Oct 25, 2026`. Build apps uses that section as the release notes, and stops if it's missing.

List only the bigger changes every user would notice. Small or technical changes go under one last line: `- Bug fixes and small improvements.`

## v0.7.0 · Nov 2, 2026

**Team owners:** re-publish the Firebase rules (rules version 10).

- **New look:** borderless buttons, cards and menus with soft shadows, hover highlights on every button, a brighter dark theme, and the glowing topo map background on every screen.
- **Much faster:** the app opens and scrolls far more smoothly, and the topo background no longer slows long inventory pages down.
- Picking your team goes straight to its sign-in form.
- Parts track how many are **damaged** and how many **broken**, shown under Condition.
- Nicer printed sheets: a brand header, page numbers, and a choice of Stylized, Basic or Compact tables.
- **Add another part** button after the last part on computers.
- Bug fixes and small improvements.

## v0.6.4 · Oct 23, 2026

**Team owners:** re-publish the Firebase rules (rules version 9).

- Custom parts: **Made by** and **Material** fields, plus ☰ → **Print list** to track parts to make, in progress and made.
- New **Damaged** condition for parts.
- QR labels: Normal, Stylized and Mini styles, copies per label, and a cleaner print menu.
- The Thrifty Bot FTC collection added to the parts catalog.
- Bug fixes and small improvements.
