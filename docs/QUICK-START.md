Our team's parts list, on any computer or phone. It works offline too: changes sync once you're back online. Print the [PDF version](QUICK-START.pdf) (one page) and post it in the shop.

**1. Get the app**

Easiest: just use the website, **[huskerthecat.github.io/FTC_Ultimate_Inventory](https://huskerthecat.github.io/FTC_Ultimate_Inventory/)**.

- **Windows:** download **[FTC-Ultimate-Inventory-Setup.exe](https://github.com/HuskerTheCat/FTC_Ultimate_Inventory/releases/latest/download/FTC-Ultimate-Inventory-Setup.exe)**. If Windows warns you, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Android:** download **[FTC-Ultimate-Inventory.apk](https://github.com/HuskerTheCat/FTC_Ultimate_Inventory/releases/latest/download/FTC-Ultimate-Inventory.apk)** and open it. Allow your browser to install apps when Android asks.
- **iPhone or iPad:** open the website in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

**2. Join the team (first time only)**

1. Get the join code or QR code from your team admin (the person who set up the team in the app).
2. Under **New to the team?**, tap **Scan QR** and scan it, or paste the code.
3. Enter your name, email and a password (8 or more characters), accept the [privacy policy](../PRIVACY.md), and tap **Create account**. Then open the link in the email you get.

On a new device later, choose **Sign in to your team**, pick your program (**FTC**), enter the team number, then sign in.

**3. Add a part**

1. Tap **Scan** at the top and scan the package's barcode. goBILDA, REV, AndyMark, ServoCity, TETRIX, Studica, SWYFT, Axon, Limelight and The Thrifty Bot parts fill in by themselves. If the team already has it, it opens instead: tap **+** for each one, then **Done**.
2. No barcode? Tap **+ Add part** and type the part number or the item name.
3. Check the quantity, pick a **Location**, and tap **Save**. Locations are set up in **☰ → Locations**.

**4. Take, return or find parts**

Tap **−** or **+** next to a part as soon as you take or return one. To find a part, type in **Search**, or tap **Scan** and scan a bin's QR label to see what's in it. Labels are printed from **⋯ → Print QR labels**.

**5. Made a mistake?**

Tap **Undo** at the bottom of the screen. It shows for 10 seconds after each change. Deleted something? It waits in **☰ → Trash** for 30 days.

More help: the [how-to guide](HOW-TO.md) and [troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md) pages, or ask your team admin.
